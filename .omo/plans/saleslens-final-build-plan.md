# saleslens-final-build-plan - Work Plan

## TL;DR (For humans)

**What you'll get:** A self-hostable OneSchema FileFeeds clone for B2B SaaS — `FileFeed: SFTP/S3/email → template (contacts/companies/opportunities or payroll/employee) → deterministic transforms → destination S3/SFTP/warehouse/API` that makes *customer* CSV onboarding map itself after first approval — zero re-verification on unchanged files (Merkle BLAKE3) and <2min audit `which rule touched this cell` — version-controlled YAML→SQL compiler + ledger, not MDM. Same 70% code as SalesLens, now sold to GTM ops whose *deal* blocks on CSV, not to the 5-person data team.

**Why this approach:** OneSchema validates pricing: FileFeeds **`$50k+/yr + importer $650/mo + 1,200/yr cap`** [wetransform.com/dromo.io] and in-house **`$100k + $75k/yr, 2× overrun 3-6mo`** [oneschema.co/blog] — budget exists because revenue stalls. Research falsified generic unification (warehouse+manual `stg_*` is $0, MDM `$1.5-4M` straw, H1 50% washed out, H4 pluggable, H5 whitespace) and validated FileFeeds narrow moat: dbt-style YAML→SQL IR + ephemeral validate, Postgres append-only ledger advisory-lock, Arrow IPC 8-64k + DuckDB aggregates. Harder moat (Sato/TURL/RECA + blocking learner) gated research, not MVP. **Pivot keeps 70% code, changes buyer from data eng to GTM.**

**What it will NOT do:** No second ER backend or BPM UI (seam WARN only), no Spark 5×, no new quality engine (wrap Soda at $0), no connector marketplace, no `stg_*` vs `revenue ($)` generic pitch — FileFeeds is customer-upload → template, not internal sales unification. For FileFeeds MVP, **Splink ER + per-col p99 drift-sentinel deferred** (customer files are contacts/companies, not 400k deduplication on day 1).

**Effort:** Large (2-3 months solo, 20 todos + 4 verifiers, P0 gates must be green; FileFeeds MVP ships Waves 1 + 3 of Wave 2 in 3-4 weeks)
**Risk:** Medium — P0 gates close 7 critique kills; remaining risk is FileFeed template coverage (`contacts/companies/opportunities` + `payroll/employee`) and `__EMPTY_3` dialect, gated by bad-triad fixture
**Decisions to sanity-check:** (1) Self-hostable `host in your own cloud` vs no EU hosting CLOUD Act [wetransform.com] — do you need EU? (2) Per-seat $0-500 vs OneSchema $50k+ — disclose `1,200/yr cap+overage` [dromo.io] (3) Keep ER deferred for FileFeeds MVP or ship Splink 20M in wave2?
